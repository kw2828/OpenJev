Bring your own decisions

The interactive playground accepts editable context, questions, and candidate descriptions. It returns each selected candidate ID, the full candidate distribution, and model metadata. Export the request and response as JSON.

Scores are uncalibrated and relative to your choices. They are not guarantees of correctness, safety, or outcome success.

Portable by design

The Linux container runs Qwen3-0.6B on CPU, the API, and the real Doom engine. That checkpoint is smaller than the Mac 4B model shown above. Public instances share a single arena and display a notice before you enter instructions.
