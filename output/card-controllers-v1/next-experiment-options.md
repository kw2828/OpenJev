# One conditional architecture experiment

**Design only, before reading the running controller comparison.** No model, native environment, or random-stream calls; no new seeds or training authorization. The original architecture pilot remains **failed, 1/6**. The present A/B/C evaluation can establish a controller effect, not retrospectively repair that result.

## Recommendation: test the locality hypothesis under one fixed controller

If C passes its frozen controller-benefit rule, the next useful architecture experiment is a **fresh, paired confirmation of innovation-local versus gain-matched diffuse writes**, with C fixed for every model and reference. Do not add a cache, change the loss, enlarge the memory, or introduce a biological name. The intervention already exists in code; the missing evidence is whether its recurrent state produces useful decisions once the shared picker is adequate. This is an architecture experiment, not a claim to have invented another architecture.

The causal contrast is narrow. From the same pre-write state, key and observation, local inflation adds `c*k²` to diagonal covariance. The diffuse control adds `c*sum(k⁴)/sum(k²)` to every coordinate. They match the immediate key-projected covariance increment and `k·gain`, but can protect different associations in subsequent states. Both retain fully differentiable residual statistics, the same shared readout and the same 32-by-16 mean state plus 32 covariance values. The hypothesis is **less interference with other previously acquired associations, leading to better native return**, not calibrated uncertainty, better exploration, or an exact Kalman posterior.

Keep all six existing families and three paired initializations: local, matched diffuse, ordinary diagonal Kalman, delta, gated delta, and GRU512. Eighteen small fits are preferable to selecting whichever conventional control looks weakest or strongest in the running comparison. Exact public-table and last-32-selected-reveal references remain visible under the same C picker. The candidate must compete with the maximum mean return of the four prespecified conventional learned families, not a favorable subset.

## A bounded independent run, if the task remains informative

Proposed scope is unchanged native ConcentrationHard: **128 new training decks, 32 new development decks, 64 new paired evaluation decks; 16 epochs, batch 16, 128 updates per fit, 2,304 updates total**. All new identities must be independently frozen and disjoint from both prior runs. Three new paired initial tensors and the same minibatch orders are shared across arms wherever schemas agree. Use one common, precollected public training corpus with the original fixed 50:50 teacher/random decision mixture, not model-specific collection.

Retain the existing pre-reveal, previously-seen/currently-hidden cross-entropy and episode weighting, Adam settings, full episode gradients, padding semantics and final checkpoint only. All 18 fits finish before development or native evaluation. Development is descriptive; no epoch, temperature, gate, threshold or checkpoint selection. Native coverage is **20 controllers by 64 decks = 1,280 episodes**, at most 133,120 native actions and 119,808 learned prediction/write calls each. C is the sole picker; no second controller sweep.

Primary proposed evidence is local-minus-matched native mean return at least **0.02**, positive in all three paired fits. Require local to exceed the strongest conventional family mean by **0.03**, with positive corresponding differences for all three pairs. These reuse the original practical effect sizes prospectively on genuinely new training and evaluation data; they do not rescore the failed pilot. Keep every fit and deck, and report paired deck differences rather than treating actions or repeated fits as independent layouts.

Also require useful retained-history performance: local must exceed last32 mean return by **0.03**, with each local fit positive against that same reference. Report the complete exact-table gap, success, pairs and action count without demanding that a learned memory beat an exact public table on a clean static-deck problem. Exact and last32 have privileged **accuracy of their stored public evidence**, not access to unrevealed cards. Neither is a learned baseline.

One saved-output mechanism check accompanies native return: on the common development histories, query all eligible previously seen hidden positions immediately before and after each actual write. Use the **same pre-write public target mask and labels** for both reads, excluding the written position; do not let the new frame change the denominator. Report the change in cross-entropy for these other stored positions, with equal episode weighting. Compare local and matched arms across all three fits. These extra queries never feed the picker or change memory and must be charged. Reduced off-key damage without a native benefit is insufficient; native benefit without reduced off-key damage does not establish the proposed interference explanation. No counterfactual native trajectories are needed.

## What is, and is not, matched

The main local/matched contrast has identical trainable parameter count, state dimensions, inputs, targets, update counts, query schedule and initialization. Its trajectories and gradients are allowed to diverge. The two additions have different arithmetic and covariance traces, so report actual work and wall time rather than promise exact compute equality. A same-input-state unit check must verify projected-gain matching; gains from diverged episode states are not expected to remain equal.

All learned arms receive the same public event history during supervised training and the same information boundary during control. Native actions can produce different later histories. The last32 reference intentionally has a shorter retained history; it is a forgetting control, not a parameter-matched neural model. The exact table is an information-preserving competence reference. GRU512 matches mean-state scalar count only and has substantially more trainable parameters; diagonal Kalman memories also retain 32 extra state scalars. Do not describe all six arms as capacity- or compute-matched.

Report active and allocated parameters, persistent tensor bytes, public seen/matched/phase metadata, actual reference buffers, scratch space, writes, all queried positions, forward/backward/optimizer calls, whole training time and whole deployment time. Count the mechanism-check queries separately. The local/matched floating state alone is 2,176 bytes in float32. A static 52-position public rank table is a strong, inexpensive alternative; no compression advantage over that table follows from calling the matrix a memory. Freeze a single measured wall/storage cap before any new allocation, without a later extension or compensating extra updates.

## Conditional decision after the current comparison

- **C passes and useful history remains exposed:** this warrants the single independent experiment above if the exact-table/last32 results show room to distinguish retained information. A broad improvement shared by all families is evidence for C, not for local covariance. Similar or saturated performance across exact, last32 and learned controllers leaves little architectural headroom; do not train another identical run merely to obtain a positive contrast.
- **C does not pass:** stop this controller/training recipe. A favorable family, B-only improvement or prediction metric does not bypass its frozen rule. Continue the broader recurrent-memory research goal only through a separately motivated task or mechanism with new evidence, not another threshold, picker or training-budget tweak here. This note does not nominate or authorize a replacement benchmark.
- **The independent architecture experiment fails:** close the local-covariance utility hypothesis at this budget and information boundary. Do not infer equivalence from a failed superiority test. Passing supports a bounded decision-memory result and justifies testing one independently chosen transfer setting; it does not establish connectome relevance, robotic world modeling, or publication novelty.

## Prior art and possible contribution

This proposal reuses the [earlier primary-source audit](../decision-memory-direction-v1/related-work.md), rather than adding a new literature search. Error-corrective associative writes are established by [DeltaNet, v6](https://arxiv.org/html/2406.06484v6#S2.SS2). Covariance-conditioned gains and diagonal precision updates are established by [Kalman Delta Networks, v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.07816v1#S4.SS1). Surprise-prioritized exact caches already overlap with [HOLA, v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2607.02303v1#S3.SS3), which is another reason not to bolt a cache onto this comparison.

The possible contribution is much narrower: evidence that a particular coordinate-local uncertainty reopening rule preserves useful associations better than an immediate-gain-matched diffuse rule, under an independently validated public decision policy, with its actual state and compute costs disclosed. That mechanism remains unproven and this small prior-art screen cannot establish novelty. The existing explicit table is a serious baseline, not an inconvenient implementation detail.
