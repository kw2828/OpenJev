Fixed-policy training collection preserved as a failed attempt. All 72 planned trajectories and 52,072 native steps completed, but three groups did not meet the predeclared row quotas. No mixture, continuation training or held-out evaluation was admitted.

The archive contains all original raw traces, failure and supervisor records, frozen plan, new source and metadata-only review. All 30 archive members were verified against SHA-256. The review checks cohort membership, episode metadata, quotas, recorded operation totals and process closure; it does not replace a numerical policy audit.
