One registered FIT-only author NL-LFR fit reached its 10,000-iteration cap. Its stopping flag remained false: **REFERENCE_INCOMPLETE**. The fixed continuation rule does not pass with an incomplete conventional reference.

All 384 diagnostic forecasts were finite and replayed exactly in the independent audit. Diagnostic standardized RMSE was 0.051458, versus 0.042901 for the unchanged recurrent residual. These values do not establish superiority over a completed nonlinear reference. Most bounded context-state solves also reached their caps. Full-request timing is descriptive, not a controlled speed comparison.
