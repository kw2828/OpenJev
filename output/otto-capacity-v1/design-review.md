# Prospective capacity-screen design review

Status: source and design review only. No new dataset, model prediction, fitting result or autonomous search was inspected or computed for this review. The [protocol](../../research/otto-capacity-protocol.md) is prospective until its source/runtime/input closure and separate qualification are frozen.

The useful comparison is nine fresh networks on identical observed teacher returns. The fresh width-eight control separates the new initialization recipe from capacity: the earlier 0.01-scale initialization and trained checkpoints cannot serve as the sole narrow control. Paired seeds and orders reduce variation but do not make different-width initial functions identical. Equal optimizer updates do not establish equal computation.

The input contract is essential. Byte-identical cached float32 features define the empirical alias floor; final regression scoring must use exactly those inputs upcast to float64. Reconstructing raw higher-precision features only for scoring could distinguish members of an alias group and invalidate that bound for the scored function. The floor measures finite-cohort label inconsistency, not population uncertainty. Common realized teacher returns make the targets identical across models, while leaving Monte Carlo noise and correlated prefixes present.

The 10% VALID and 20% above-floor TRAIN improvements are reasonable fixed effect-size screens, but have no supplied power calculation. Requiring both metrics for every seed and both wider families is intentionally strict. Near-zero narrow TRAIN excess makes a relative threshold numerically sensitive and potentially uninformative; retain the signed value and original inequality rather than adding an epsilon or switching gates. All twelve cells and absolute errors must remain visible.

The qualification now uses 256 fixed TRAIN rows and batch 128, matching the full fitting batch geometry: six epochs produce twelve updates per family and 36 overall. The projection scales all three paid fit times by `3*(3520/12)`, including their fixed initialization/export overhead. It is still an estimate under changing cache, memory and runtime conditions, not a guaranteed upper bound. The 120-second qualification cap, 1,200-second projected fitting bound and 1,800-second full hard cap are separate requirements. No late runtime or batch-size change is an acceptable implicit retry.

The primary reference supports investigating capacity: the larger isotropic benchmark used three 1,024-unit hidden layers. Its Bellman learning, exploration and observation model differ from this fixed Monte Carlo regression screen. Width 128 is a bounded diagnostic, not a reproduction of that result. [Loisy and Heinonen, Section 3 and Appendix C, Table 5](https://auroreloisy.github.io/papers/Loisy2023a_EurPhysJE_drl-benchmark.pdf).

Even a complete twelve-condition pass establishes only improved fixed-data scalar fitting. It does not answer branch action ranking, student-state coverage, autonomous success, recurrent-state quality or connectome efficacy. Those claims require a new matched and prospectively frozen control study; all earlier failed gates remain unchanged.
