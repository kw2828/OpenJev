# Next architecture review: spatial interactions before value compression

Status: a conditional research suggestion, not a frozen experiment or an efficacy claim. This review used closed historical reports and public primary papers. It did not inspect the active conditioning-control run, decode scientific arrays, or call a model or simulator.

The most useful next architectural question is whether **nonlinear local spatial processing before global compression improves deployed action ranking**. The present scalar readout reduces 11,025 spatial entries to eight linear measurements before its first nonlinearity. Increasing width or adding recurrence is not a substitute for testing that specific mechanism. Exact public filtering already supplies the posterior; position and the known observation kernel are also supplied. A finite-horizon optimal controller would additionally need remaining time, so belief sufficiency should not be overstated. Any added public context must be shared by every comparator.

The closed evidence narrows, but does not answer, this question:

- The [scalar study](../../research/otto-return-value-results.md) failed **0/54**. The minimum-of-eight-planes model had lower scalar VALID MSE than the ordinary MLP but markedly worse autonomous success. Concavity, homogeneity and lower regression error are therefore insufficient explanations or admissions by themselves.
- The [capacity screen](../../research/otto-capacity-results.md) passed **6/12**, all TRAIN conditions and no required VALID conditions. Width 128 lowered mean VALID MSE by **7.93%**; the deeper model raised it by **1.37%**. This rejects the fixed recipe, not all larger networks, and supplies no autonomous result.
- [Bellman continuation](../../research/otto-bellman-control-results.md) passed **19/42**, including **0/18 competence**. It improved over matched Monte Carlo continuation but remained far behind analytic control and below the unchanged family on lambda5. Targets and coverage remain competing explanations.
- The [coverage collection](../../research/otto-coverage-collection-results.md) completed 72 trajectories but failed its fixed mixture quota. No mixed-data training ran. The separate [symmetry study](../../research/otto-symmetry-head-results.md) already tested shared action frames and pooled student data and failed **2/54**; simply repeating D4 sharing is not a new mechanism.
- [Fixed conditioning](../../research/otto-conditioning-results.md) passed **3/6 scalar conditions**: mean TRAIN excess fell **42.11%**, while mean VALID MSE rose **9.73%**. Its autonomous question remains outside this review.

## A small falsifiable comparison

Refine the existing [spatial-readout hypothesis](../../research/otto-spatial-readout-hypothesis.md), rather than present it as new prior art. For each already constructed branch belief `z`, form fixed local mass sums with zero-padded square windows of widths **3, 9 and 27**. At each cell, a small shared nonlinear function sees its mass, these local sums, relative coordinates, publicly derivable boundary geometry and supplied sensing length. Probability-weight its outputs, sum over cells, and apply a small signed scalar readout with the existing raw `c0 * mass` baseline. This can distinguish clustered versus separated mass before reducing the map to a short vector. It is a hypothesis about useful interactions, not proof that discarded information caused past failures.

Use three fresh paired-seed families: **the spatial-sum model; an ordinary small CNN with the same geometry/context and comparable receptive field; the spatial-sum model with neighborhood inputs removed**. Retain the frozen ordinary MLP as a historical reference and analytic control as the competence anchor. Fix widths, scaling and learning recipe prospectively; report active parameters and measured computation separately. Equal updates or nominal channel counts do not establish equal compute. A fresh parameter-matched dense control would be necessary before claiming an advantage specifically over dense architectures.

All trained families should use the same authenticated 5,589 TRAIN rows, uniform Monte Carlo targets, seed orders and final-checkpoint schedule. Keep exposed VALID descriptive. Keep identical explicit sixteen-branch construction, mass floor, signed values, physical factor of 64, legal actions and ties. Do not introduce candidate-only normalization, zero-input repair, homogeneity, positivity, auxiliary ranking targets or per-model Bellman target streams. If any such change is desired, give it to every architecture or test it separately.

The hypothesis loses support if the neighbor-free model matches it, an ordinary CNN explains its improvement, or lower scalar error fails to improve fresh paired autonomous searches. A useful result must meet absolute analytic-relative competence, not merely beat a failing MLP, and retain all seeds, settings, censored searches and full branch/readout/update/setup costs. Local processing across sixteen 105x105 maps can be costly despite few weights; no speed claim follows from parameter count. Exact thresholds and a resource-qualified execution plan remain future work.

Connectome-style sparsity is a later, separate question. A biological mask needs a specified mapping from neurons to this computation and comparison with degree-preserving rewiring, random sparse masks, and dense/task-local alternatives at stated parameter and measured-cost budgets. Any gain could reflect locality or regularization. Neither sparse wiring, a generic CNN, shared pooling nor this combination establishes biological fidelity or architectural novelty.
