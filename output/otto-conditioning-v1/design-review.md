# Conditioning control: prospective design review

**Source- and literature-based review only. No dataset, model, training or simulator execution.** The [protocol](../../research/otto-conditioning-protocol.md) fixes the next scalar control; executable qualification, study and later autonomous plans remain separate requirements. Historical sources and failed attempts are unchanged.

The missing control is ordinary optimization under a different input parameterization. The same width-eight function can start approximately unchanged when spatial inputs are multiplied by 53 and their incoming weights divided by 53. This preserves width, targets, row exposure, parameter count and context information while permitting a different learning trajectory. Raw mass must feed the unchanged `c0` baseline; using scaled mass there would change the initial function and confound the comparison.

The fixed gain has a geometric rationale: a uniform belief over 2,809 possible source cells has L2 norm 1/53. The padded 105x105 representation does not make the support 11,025 cells. No empirical claim about the actual training distribution follows from this calculation. Neither gain is fitted to VALID, and no alternatives are searched. Input conditioning itself is established practice, so success would provide an optimization baseline, not novelty. [Efficient Backprop](https://leon.bottou.org/publications/pdf/tricks-1998.pdf).

The contrast does not isolate conditioning from all optimizer effects. Adam approximately cancels uniform coordinate gradient scaling in its moment ratio, but identical parameter-space steps in inverse-scaled coordinates need not preserve function-space steps. Finite epsilon, global gradient clipping and finite precision add differences. Changed implicit regularization is part of the tested recipe. Save pre-clip norms and paid costs already produced by the training loop; do not reinterpret a pass as a specific causal diagnosis. [Adam, Algorithm 1 and Section 2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1412.6980v9).

The initialization control is stronger than merely matching seeds: require elementwise float64 function agreement at `1e-6 + 1e-6*abs(gain1)` across every selected TRAIN input plus zero/half fixtures, with all three full-study pairs qualified before any optimizer update. Float32 inverse scaling prevents a byte-identity claim. Final float64 export/Torch-double parity uses the stricter fixed 1e-10 absolute/relative bound, with first-eight TRAIN/VALID and zero/half inputs in the full stage. All scoring starts from the original cached float32 input and applies the gain after upcasting, as specified.

Both arms use the existing finite-cohort alias floor. Scaling cannot separate raw-identical inputs, so it remains a lower bound; floating-point multiplication is not asserted to be an exact bijection. Signed excess is reported without repair. Teacher Monte Carlo labels remain noisy policy returns, and the correlated prefixes are not independent samples. VALID has already been exposed. Consequently the six conditions test scalar fitting on this dataset, not action ranking or search competence.

Qualification uses one paired seed, 256 TRAIN rows, the actual batch size 128 and 24 total updates. The two fit times project to the six-fit workload by `3*sum(fit_seconds)*3520/12`, with a fixed 400-second threshold and 600-second full cap. The estimate scales fixed fit overhead and cannot guarantee future timing. The scientific work remains all six fresh fits, 80 epochs and 21,120 updates, with no fitted-checkpoint reuse or validation choice.

The [capacity result](../../research/otto-capacity-results.md) and [Bellman result](../../research/otto-bellman-control-results.md) do not identify scaling as the cause of poor policies. Capacity passed 6/12 conditions; Bellman passed 19/42 and failed overall. The [coverage attempt](../../research/otto-coverage-collection-results.md) failed a sampling quota, leaving its learning comparison unanswered. This lower-cost control therefore precedes coverage without rejecting it or ruling out spatial readout, recurrence or connectome hypotheses.

The critical design commitment is to retain both gains for a separately frozen fresh autonomous comparison after technical completion and independent audit, **whether or not the scalar screen passes**. All three seeds, lambda3/4/5, matched cases, analytic control and complete deployment costs are necessary. A scalar pass alone establishes no policy benefit; a miss does not justify avoiding a control test that could reveal different action ranking. No autonomous seeds, gates or results are invented by this review.
