Six direct readout solves completed, with twelve TRAIN export checks and fifteen development views. The independent audit passed; the fixed ridge candidate failed its performance rule, passing 4 of 6 seed/setting comparisons.

Ridge lowered mean later decision gap 16.49% versus Adam74 in lambda3, but was 3.71% worse in lambda4. Both failing cases are retained. All methods were evaluated on the same already exposed 36 DEV paths, held out from fitting but not fresh confirmation.

Each direct adaptation took 4.79-5.16 seconds, including full shared feature/design costs, its solve, export and ordinary TRAIN validation. This is not a matched speed benchmark against historical Adam, and inference uses the same recurrent network. The result strengthens a conventional baseline; it establishes no new architecture, autonomous improvement or calibrated probabilities.
