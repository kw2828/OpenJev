## Result

The persistent-memory correction experiment completed all 90 trajectories and twelve final fits. Its fixed continuation rule **failed: 40 of 53 conditions passed**. The proposed correction trails an ordinary persistent GRU on primary agreement and teacher-score gap in both settings. The explicit-error GRU has the lowest mean gap among learned families.

These are forecasts on recorded paths, not autonomous control, calibrated probabilities, a new RL algorithm or evidence of architectural novelty. Primary metrics retain zero-support episodes in the denominator. All seeds and both settings remain reported.
