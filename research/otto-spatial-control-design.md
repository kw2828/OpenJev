# Outcome-blind autonomous spatial comparison

This is an unfrozen design recommendation. It was written from the [fixed training protocol](otto-spatial-training-protocol.md), qualified source and [published synthetic timings](otto-spatial-qualification-results.md), without reading current training outputs or predictions. It does not authorize a run. Successful completion and audit of all fifteen fixed final checkpoints, followed by deployed-branch qualification, are prerequisites. Scalar ranking must not select an arm, seed, checkpoint or setting.

## Fixed comparison

Keep all three fitting seeds, 10101/10102/10103, for `spatial`, `neighbor_free`, `cnn`, `dense128` and `statistics`, plus the unchanged `analytic_inbounds` controller. The candidate is prospectively `spatial`. Neighbor-free tests the value of neighborhood features; CNN, dense and statistics are ordinary alternative representations/capacities. None is a novel architecture claim by itself.

Use 24 environmental cases per supplied sensing length 3, 4 and 5, on the unchanged 53x53 board with four hit categories, `R_dt=2` and horizon 2,188. For case `c`, initial hit is `1+c%3` and block is `c//3`. This preserves eight complete three-stratum blocks, rather than weakening the previous 6/8-block comparison. There are **72 paired environmental cases and 1,152 episodes**. Fitting seeds are repeated policies on the same cases, not additional independent environmental samples.

Proposed episode ranges are 16100001-16100024, 16200001-16200024 and 16300001-16300024, with template seeds 16500001-16500003. These are reservations only: a separate scoped seed-ledger review must verify nonoverlap before freeze. The source-only search performed for this note is not a freshness certificate. Use seed-outer/family-inner arm order followed by analytic, rotated by `(regime_index*24+case)%16`. Pair sources and channel/index uniforms across arms, without forcing the same observations after paths diverge.

## Faithful deployment and qualification

Reuse the qualified public posterior view, exact kernel and native/public update contract. Keep source coordinates, random generator state and native posterior out of actor inputs. Preserve final found and censored updates, the full horizon on failure, and in-bounds action selection. There is no stuck stop, fallback or anti-cycle override.

Use `rl_branches` to construct all sixteen explicit nonfound successors, including the raw costs for blocked actions. Pass centered float64 branch inputs, integer successor positions and the supplied sensing length directly to each immutable spatial `FrozenValue`. Do not reconstruct metadata from mass-scaled features, normalize again, clip signed values, zero biased empty-branch outputs, or fuse away floored branches. Physical values remain 64 times normalized values; costs retain the qualified mass floor and first eligible ID strictly within `1e-10` of the minimum.

Before autonomous calls, qualify every final head against a Torch64 copy of its saved float32 parameters. Use the same first eight TRAIN and first eight VALID public prefixes for every head, plus 36 predeclared synthetic tuples: sensing lengths 3/4/5, positions center/(0,0)/(52,52), and four fixed beliefs (asymmetric normalized, source-successor point mass, zero, subfloor). Specify the exact synthetic arrays before checkpoint decoding. Their purpose is numerical coverage, not efficacy.

This is 52 tuples per head, **780 sixteen-row NumPy calls and 780 Torch64 calls**, plus fifteen loads and fifteen restorations; zero simulator calls or fitting. Store the 52 shared tuple arrays once, with per-head references and input hashes. One shared centered sixteen-branch view occupies about 73.4 MB; two views occupy about 146.8 MB. Repeating them for every head would exceed the allocation and is forbidden. Save per-head physical values, four costs, masks and choices; shared branch masses and weights also need only one copy. A proposed fixed numerical predicate is `abs(a-b) <= 1e-8 + 1e-10*abs(b)` on physical values and costs, plus exact selected-action agreement. For physical branch values, the absolute term is deliberately 64 times stricter in normalized units than the training study's `1e-8` absolute term; this is a separate proposed deployment contract, not a restatement of that study's qualification. Review and freeze it before running, and never relax it after seeing failures. Allow 600 seconds, 4 GiB RSS and 256 MiB output for qualification and its separately counted saved replay. Full evaluation consumes this completed qualification and does not repeat it.

## Utility and cost decisions

Compute each setting's mixture-weighted means from its authenticated positive-initial-hit weights, with eight cases per stratum. Report all 48 arm/setting cells, all fifteen heads, raw found/censored counts, strata and eight block differences. Family means average the three fitting seeds equally. Never pool lambda5 failure away; it tests supplied-model parameter extrapolation on the same board, not adaptation to unknown dynamics.

Retain the fixed absolute competence anchor for every spatial seed and setting: weighted success at least 0.95 and capped moves at most 1.05 times analytic. These are eighteen conditions. Publish the identical checks for all other learned heads descriptively.

For a neighborhood/representation continuation screen, require all four comparisons against each of the four learned control families, separately in every setting: no success regression; at least 5% lower capped moves; strictly positive paired move gains in at least 6/8 blocks; and no greater complete controller seconds per search. That adds 48 conditions, giving **66 candidate conditions**. These are practical screening rules, not calibrated significance tests. Failure remains informative without selecting another family afterward.

Also show the analytic utility/cost reference explicitly. Passing the learned-control screen does not establish an efficiency gain over analytic control. Such a claim requires the corresponding measured success, moves and complete cost to support it. A positive result would justify a separate environment/confirmation study, not establish novelty, optimality or a recurrent/connectome mechanism.

## Realistic allocation and evidence

The maximum is **2,520,576 native steps**, **1,155 resets** including three templates, and **2,363,040 learned forwards** evaluating 37,808,640 branch rows. There are 1,080 learned episodes. Load each head once and allocate that physical load over its 72 searches; allocate shared learned-module setup over 1,080 searches. For per-head amortization, that is one-fifteenth of shared setup, not one-fifteenth charged again on every search. Charge actor initialization, all branch/feature/centering work, prediction, reduction/selection and every public update. Keep original fitting, qualification, templates/native steps, I/O and parent/worker timing scopes explicit. Record amortization at H=1/100/10000 using each head's actual fit cost and preparation/15, without adding nested times twice.

Published maximum synthetic head times imply approximately **8,700 seconds for head inference alone** if all learned episodes reach the horizon. They exclude branch/filter/native work and are not predicted episode lengths. Recommend a prospective **21,600-second CPU1 worker cap, 8 GiB RSS and 12 GiB output**, with streaming traces. A 5,400- or 7,200-second worker cap would depend on favorable early termination. These remain hard stop allocations, not completion guarantees. If six hours is unacceptable, revise the cohort openly before any autonomous outputs, rather than truncate arms or extend a live run.

The 12 GiB output proposal still requires a prospective byte projection from the final serialized schema. The inherited learned step contains 52 branch/cost floats (sixteen raw masses, sixteen weights, sixteen values, four costs), two posterior witnesses, public packet, timing and identity fields; add both operation attempt/return pairs and the draw evidence duplicated in raw and allocated episode records. A provisional **4 KiB per native decision across all files** gives about **9.62 GiB** at the full step cap, leaving 2.38 GiB for summaries, reset/setup records and variance. This is a planning allowance, not a measured or guaranteed bound. Before freeze, serialize fabricated maximal-length records under the actual writer and multiply their total by the full allocation; account for all journals and duplicate records. Increase or revise the declared cap before launch if necessary. Store posterior hashes and the required small branch witnesses in full-control traces, never expanded centered branch arrays per decision.

Preserve all raw public transitions, evaluator-only pairing evidence, branch values/costs, posterior hashes, masks, operation journals and paid timings. An independent saved audit should have its own **21,600-second, 4 GiB RSS, 1 GiB output** allocation: replaying the same expensive heads cannot credibly retain the previous 1,800-second cap under the all-censored envelope. Count every saved-head replay. At the maximum, 2,363,040 forwards produce 4,726,080 attempt/return events. The former 256 MiB proposal would not accommodate ordinary verbose JSONL records. The revised 1 GiB cap permits about 227 bytes per event before other files, so a fabricated serialization projection of the final journal schema and summaries is still required before freeze; compact records may be necessary. Shared qualified NumPy network algebra must be disclosed; independently reconstruct public filtering, branch geometry, arithmetic, complete coverage and all 66 conditions. No new environments or optimizer calls occur in that audit. Publish only after successful original process closure and agreement.
