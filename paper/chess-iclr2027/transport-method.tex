\section{Two-endpoint relation transport}
The earlier candidate comparison motivates a different question: can a move
head use long-range root-board relations without recomputing a spatial network
for every successor? We implement two-endpoint transport as a controlled
representation hypothesis. It uses no afterstate, teacher shortlist or search.

\paragraph{Mechanism.}
Freeze the three direct-policy backbones from the candidate experiment. For
each legal move, initialize a width-32 query from its existing source,
destination, pooled-board and promotion/displacement features. Initialize two
distributions $p^0$ and $r^0$ as point masses at the move's endpoints. Four
row-stochastic matrices $P_k$ represent mover attacks, their reverse,
opponent attacks and their reverse. Pinned pieces retain geometric attacks;
empty rows become self-loops. At each of three internal steps, the query
produces separate four-way softmax gates $\alpha_k^t$ and $\beta_k^t$:
\begin{equation}
 p^{t+1}=\sum_{k=1}^{4}\alpha_k^t p^tP_k,
 \qquad r^{t+1}=\sum_{k=1}^{4}\beta_k^t r^tP_k.
\end{equation}
Pool frozen node features under both distributions and their normalized
pointwise product, retaining the product's mass as another scalar. A shared
GRU updates the query from these pools. A zero-initialized scalar output adds a
correction to the backbone logit. All arms therefore start with identical
predictions. The head stores 16,744 parameters; dense batched operations process
the legal menu. The overlap represents root-graph accessibility, not consequences
created by the move. Unattacked destinations may be absorbing.

\paragraph{Attribution controls and budget.}
Five heads compare full transport, zeroed overlap pool/mass, fixed uniform
relation gates, stationary endpoint distributions, and full transport on
degree-preserving rewired root graphs. The unchanged backbone is an additional
reference. Every color-specific rewire preserves each node's incoming and
outgoing degrees, forbids duplicate/self edges, and requests five accepted
swaps per edge under a fixed attempt ceiling. The prepared-input audit verifies
all 32,768 training roots and 65,536 rewired relations. Every training relation
reaches its swap target and changes; about 22.7\% of original edges remain.
This sampler is not uniform over all degree-matched graphs, and only one fixed
FEN-derived rewire is used per root.

Each of fifteen heads trains for six epochs and 1,536 updates on the same
32,768 old training roots, with paired seeds, minibatches, Adam settings and
legal-move cross-entropy. Backbones remain frozen; their pretraining cost is
retained. All fits finish before evaluation. Controls store equal parameters,
but inactive gates/input coordinates and different operations preclude claims
of equal active capacity or FLOPs. Both evaluation panels are already exposed
development data. The fixed continuation rule requires mean agreement gains
of one point over uniform/static/rewired, half a point over no-overlap, and no
loss against the backbone on both panels, with no paired-seed deficit worse
than half a point. This teacher-imitation screen cannot establish engine-quality
or gameplay improvements.

\paragraph{Simpler-head follow-up.}
A separately frozen nine-fit comparison adds a generic move MLP, an NCN-inspired
endpoint-product/common-neighbor head, and a typed overlap head. All use the
same frozen backbones, action features, old training data and 1,536 updates.
The generic head has two 80-unit hidden layers; the untyped overlap head uses
65. The typed head pools all sixteen ordered pairs of attack/reverse-attack
relations, projects each pooled vector from 32 to 8 dimensions with shared
weights, and uses two 48-unit hidden layers. Head counts are within 4\% of
transport. All residual outputs start at zero. No test outcomes determine
these widths. Untyped pooling uses an undirected attack union; typed pooling
preserves direction and color. These are chess adaptations of structural
pooling, not reproductions of the original NCN encoder and benchmark.

All nine fits finish before evaluation. Recomputing every training-root cache
for all three backbones matches the original cache exactly. Original backbone
and transport predictions are copied with authenticated provenance; they are
not counted as fresh inference. The additional rule requires transport to gain
at least one agreement point over each new head on both panels, with no paired
seed deficit worse than half a point.

\paragraph{Joint representation training.}
A separately frozen follow-up allows gradients through the root encoder,
recurrent core, action embeddings and direct policy head. The five transport
variants and a direct policy start from the same original paired backbones;
transport heads are fresh and initially output zero. Unused value, auxiliary
and action-plane projection modules remain fixed. Every arm receives six more
epochs of legal-move cross-entropy at the same fixed learning rate and minibatch
order. Eighteen final fits total 27,648 updates, with all training completed
before evaluation. The direct comparator controls for additional backbone
training; parameters and FLOPs are not equal across arms.

The fixed criterion requires at least one agreement point over jointly trained
direct/uniform/static/rewired, half a point over no-overlap, and no loss against
the unchanged backbone on both old panels. Every paired-seed deficit must be
at most half a point. This tests a fixed optimization budget, not individually
tuned learning rates or an untouched confirmation distribution.

\paragraph{Prior-work boundary.}
Learned relation-operator mixtures are established in Neural LP
\citep{yang2017}; NBFNet constructs source/query-conditioned pair representations
through learned path aggregation \citep{zhu2021}. Neural Common Neighbor uses
structural overlap to guide learned feature pooling \citep{wang2024}. The
present head combines related ingredients for chess decisions.
